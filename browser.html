Built for the Age of AI Agents

Why traditional browsers with brittle extensions can't match a natively AI-controlled browser.

🧠

Native Agent Bridge

Direct bi-directional WebSocket and DOM accessibility tree integration allows Thoryn agents to interact with web pages with human-like precision and 10x faster response times.

📑

Parallel Multi-Tab Synthesis

Instruct your agent to research across 20 websites simultaneously. Thoryn Browser gathers, cross-checks, and synthesizes the findings into clean summaries and printable reports.

Local Session Vault

Your login cookies, passwords, and sessions remain stored securely on your local device with AES-256 encryption. Raw credentials are never transmitted to cloud servers.
